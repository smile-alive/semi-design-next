## API Reference

<Framework only="react">

<BannerReactApi />

Other native attributes (such as `aria-*`, `onClick` and `id`) land on the root `div` element. The root's `role="alert"` is fixed and cannot be overridden.

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

<BannerVueApi />

`class`, `style` and native attributes are passed through attrs and land on the root element; the root's `role="alert"` is fixed and cannot be overridden. `title` is also a string prop, and the `#title` slot wins when both are given. Content of `0` in `title`, `description` and the default slot is rendered. The closed state lives inside the component and has no v-model; unmount and mount again with `v-if` to show it again.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

<BannerSolidApi />

`class`, `style`, `ref` and native attributes and events (such as `aria-*` and `onClick`) land on the root element (`role="alert"`). The root's `role` is fixed and cannot be overridden. The closed state lives inside the component; unmount and mount again to show it again.

</Framework>

<Framework only="svelte">

<BannerSvelteApi />

`class`, `style` (a string) and native attributes and events (such as `aria-*` and `onclick`) land on the root element (`role="alert"`). The root's `role` is fixed and cannot be overridden. `title` can be a string or a snippet, and `description`, `icon` and `closeIcon` are snippets. The closed state lives inside the component and there is no `bind:`; unmount with `{#if}` and mount again to show it again.

</Framework>

## Accessibility

### ARIA

- The component has a `role` of 'alert'.
- The close button's `aria-label` comes from the current locale ('Close' in English, '关闭' in Simplified Chinese).

### Keyboard and Focus

- The close button of the Banner can be focused with the `Tab` key. After the button is focused, hit the `Enter` key or the `Space` key to close the banner.

## Content Guidelines

- Full screen Banner
    - Try to keep the content displayed completely on one line
    - Use correct punctuation, commas within sentences and periods between sentences
- Non-fullscreen Banner
    - title
        - Instructions in condensed language
        - Try to avoid using commas, periods and other punctuation marks in the title, and support the use of question marks at the end when there are and only interrogative sentences
    - text
        - On the premise of complete information transmission, try to compress the text to 1-2 sentences
        - A detailed description or explanation of the title, rather than a repetition of the title
        - Use correct punctuation, commas within sentences and periods between sentences
