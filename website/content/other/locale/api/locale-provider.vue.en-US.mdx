| locale | Locale pack. When omitted, undefined or null, the language of the outer LocaleProvider is inherited; without any outer provider, components fall back to zh_CN. The locale of ConfigProvider takes precedence over it. Takes the built-in zh_CN or en_US, or a custom pack with at least code, whose missing text and currency fall back to en_US. | `LocaleInput \| null` | `undefined` |
