### Custom Arrow

<Framework only="react">

Customize arrow styles and click events through the arrowProps property

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

Customize the attributes of the arrow buttons (`aria-label`, `style`, `onClick` and so on) through `arrowProps`; your `onClick` runs before the built-in handler and your `class` is merged with the built-in classes. Use the `#leftArrow` and `#rightArrow` slots for the icons; an empty slot leaves the button empty.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

Pass the arrow icons with the `leftArrow` and `rightArrow` props; `null` leaves the button empty. Pass the button's native attributes (such as `aria-label`) as `props` of `leftArrow` or `rightArrow` in `arrowProps`.

</Framework>

<Framework only="svelte">

Customize the attributes of the arrow buttons (`aria-label`, `style`, `onclick` and so on) through `leftArrow.props` and `rightArrow.props` of `arrowProps`; your `onclick` runs before the built-in handler and your `class` is merged with the built-in classes. Use the `leftArrow` and `rightArrow` snippets for the icons; an empty snippet leaves the button empty.

</Framework>

### Play Parameters

Pass the parameter interval to autoPlay to control the time interval between two pictures, and pass hoverToPause to control whether to stop playing when the mouse is placed on the picture

A carousel mounted with no more than one slide does not play; when slides are added later and there are more than one, it starts playing according to `autoPlay`.

<Demo id="auto-play" />

### Animation and Speed

Control the animation by giving the animation property, optional values are `fade`, `slide`  
Control the switching time between two pictures by giving the speed attribute, the unit is ms

## Accessibility

Carousel follows the [WAI-ARIA Carousel](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/) pattern.

### ARIA

- The root is `role="region"` with `aria-roledescription` "carousel". A region needs a name to become a landmark, so describe what the carousel shows with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- With indicators, each slide is the `tabpanel` of its indicator (`tab`), linked by `aria-controls` and `aria-labelledby`; an `id` already on a slide is kept. Without indicators, each slide is `role="group"` with `aria-roledescription` "slide" and the name "Slide N".
- Slides other than the current one are `inert`: their content cannot be focused and screen readers skip it. A slide must be an element or a component that puts props such as `inert`, `role` and `id` on its DOM, just like the class name and style.
- The slides container is `aria-live="off"` while rotating and `polite` otherwise, so a slide the user switches to is announced and an automatic switch is not.
- The arrow buttons are named from the locale ("Previous index", "Next index") by default; custom arrow content with readable text (text, or an element with `aria-label`) names the button instead; a name passed with `arrowProps.leftArrow.props['aria-label']` or its right counterpart wins over both.

### Keyboard and Focus

- Keyboard focus inside the carousel (an arrow, an indicator or focusable content in a slide) pauses autoplay until focus leaves, after which autoplay resumes (the APG advises staying stopped, which this differs from), and switching from the keyboard meanwhile does not restart it. A mouse click on an arrow does not pause.
- The previous and next arrows are native `button` elements: focus them with `Tab` and activate them with `Enter` or `Space`.
- The indicators form a `tablist` in which only the current one is in the `Tab` sequence. While focus is on an indicator:
    - `ArrowLeft` goes to the previous slide and `ArrowRight` to the next one, wrapping around at both ends, and focus follows to the new current indicator. In RTL the directions are swapped.
    - `Home` goes to the first slide and `End` to the last one.
