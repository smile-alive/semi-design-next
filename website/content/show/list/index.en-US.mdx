## Accessibility

Pass `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` to List to describe the list; the root element then gets `role="group"`.

<Framework only="react">

A List.Item with `onClick` or `onRightClick` (including those set on the List) stays an `<li>` list item and renders a native `<button type="button">` inside it as its only focus target, which screen readers announce as a button.

- The button wraps `header` and `main` (or `children` when there are none) and its click area covers the whole row. After tabbing to it, Enter or Space triggers `onClick` and the context-menu key triggers `onRightClick`
- `extra` is rendered outside the button on a higher layer, so buttons and links in it can be focused and clicked normally, and clicking them does not trigger the Item's `onClick`
- When `children` is combined with `header` or `main`, `children` sits outside the button and is covered by the full-row button, so put interactive content in `extra`
- A List.Item without a click callback renders no button and is not focusable

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

A List.Item with `@click` or `@rightClick` (including those set on the List) stays an `<li>` list item and renders a native `<button type="button">` inside it as its only focus target, which screen readers announce as a button.

- The button wraps the content of the `#header` and `#main` slots (or the default slot when there are none) and its click area covers the whole row. After tabbing to it, Enter or Space triggers `@click` and the context-menu key triggers `@rightClick`
- `#extra` is rendered outside the button on a higher layer, so buttons and links in it can be focused and clicked normally, and clicking them does not trigger the Item's `@click`
- When the default slot is combined with `#header` or `#main`, the default slot sits outside the button and is covered by the full-row button, so put interactive content in `#extra`
- A List.Item without a click callback renders no button and is not focusable

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

A List.Item with `onClick` or `onRightClick` (including those set on the List) stays an `<li>` list item and renders a native `<button type="button">` inside it as its only focus target, which screen readers announce as a button.

- The button wraps `header` and `main` (or `children` when there are none) and its click area covers the whole row. After tabbing to it, Enter or Space triggers `onClick` and the context-menu key triggers `onRightClick`
- `extra` is rendered outside the button on a higher layer, so buttons and links in it can be focused and clicked normally, and clicking them does not trigger the Item's `onClick`
- When `children` is combined with `header` or `main`, `children` sits outside the button and is covered by the full-row button, so put interactive content in `extra`
- A List.Item without a click callback renders no button and is not focusable

</Framework>

A List.Item used outside a List renders as a plain list item without `grid`. With `grid`, the Row that holds the items has `role="list"` and the Col around each item has `role="none"`, so screen readers read the items as members of that list.

## API reference

### List

<Framework only="react">

<ListReactApi />

Other native attributes (such as `data-*` and `id`) land on the root element; when `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` is passed without a `role`, the root element gets `role="group"`. `onClick` and `onRightClick` act on the items, not on the root element.

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

<ListVueApi />

`class`, `style` and native attributes fall through to the root element; when `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` is passed without a `role`, the root element gets `role="group"`. The payload of `@click` and `@rightClick` is the native `MouseEvent`, and they act on the items, not on the root element.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

<ListSolidApi />

`class`, `style`, `ref` and native attributes (such as `data-*`) land on the root element; when `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` is passed without a `role`, the root element gets `role="group"`. The payload of `onClick` and `onRightClick` is the native `MouseEvent`, and they act on the items, not on the root element.

</Framework>

### List grid props

<Framework only="react">

`grid` accepts only the Row props `align`, `gutter`, `justify`, `type` and the Col props `span`, `offset`, `order`, `push`, `pull`, the responsive breakpoints, `className` and `style`; other div attributes are not accepted. Refer to [Grid](/en-US/react/basic/grid) for their meaning.

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

`grid` accepts only the Row props `align`, `gutter`, `justify`, `type` and the Col props `span`, `offset`, `order`, `push`, `pull`, the responsive breakpoints, `class` and `style`; other attributes are not accepted. `class` and `style` land on the Col around each item. Refer to [Grid](/en-US/react/basic/grid) for their meaning.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

`grid` accepts only the Row props `align`, `gutter`, `justify`, `type` and the Col props `span`, `offset`, `order`, `push`, `pull`, the responsive breakpoints, `class` and `style`; other attributes are not accepted. `class` and `style` land on the Col around each item. Refer to [Grid](/en-US/react/basic/grid) for their meaning.

</Framework>

### List.Item

<Framework only="react">

<ListItemReactApi />

Other native attributes (such as `data-*`) land on the `li`; when clickable, `aria-*` and `tabIndex` land on the inner button instead. `onClick` is applied to the `li` and takes precedence over the List's `onClick`.

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

<ListItemVueApi />

`class`, `style`, `data-*`, `aria-*` and native attributes fall through to the `li`, not to the outer Col in grid mode.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

<ListItemSolidApi />

`class`, `style`, `ref` and native attributes (such as `data-*`) land on the `li`, not on the outer Col in grid mode. When clickable, `aria-*` and `tabIndex` land on the inner button instead, and `ref.focus()` moves focus to that button.

</Framework>
