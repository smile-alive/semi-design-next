## Demos

### How to import

<Framework only="react">

```jsx
import { Steps } from '@fibrae/semi-ui-react';
const Step = Steps.Step;
```

Steps treats every child element as one step position in order (text is skipped), and fill columns count them all. A component that wraps Step only needs to pass its props on to Steps.Step to be numbered; a native element takes a position and renders as is.

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
    import { Steps } from '@fibrae/semi-ui-vue';
</script>
```

`Steps.Step` can also be imported by name as `Step`. The `title`, `description` and `icon` of a Step are slots (`#title`, `#description`, `#icon`). Steps only recognizes Steps.Step elements that are its direct children; any other child is not rendered, takes no step number, and logs a warning in development. A Step renders nothing outside Steps.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

```jsx
import { Steps } from '@fibrae/semi-ui-solid';
```

Only Steps.Step takes a step position: other children, including native elements and text, are not rendered and take no number, and a development warning is logged. A Step renders nothing outside Steps.

</Framework>

### Nav Steps

You can use type="nav" to set the navigation style step bar. The navigation style step bar has the following characteristics:

1. The step bar supports interaction as well: when Steps listens for step changes or a Step has a click handler, steps are focusable and clickable, the same as the other types.

2. It is suitable when the steps are not related to each other, the content does not affect each other, and the visual elements of the page need to be highlighted.

3. The width of the step bar is opened according to the content.

4. Steps.Step only renders the title; `description`, `icon` and `status` have no effect. The other attributes and events (`aria-label`, `role`, click and keyboard handlers, class name and style, etc.) work as in the other types.

### onChange CallBack

<Framework only="react">

onChange can be used to realize the processing progress. onChange receives a parameter of type number, equal to initial plus the index of the selected step; it is not called when the current step is clicked.

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

Listen for step changes with `@change` (`onChange` of Steps is a function prop, and whether it is bound decides if the steps are clickable). The callback receives a number, equal to initial plus the index of the selected step; it is not called when the current step is clicked. `current` also supports `v-model:current`, and binding it makes every step clickable as well.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

Listen for step changes with `onChange`. The callback receives a number, equal to initial plus the index of the selected step; it is not called when the current step is clicked. Passing `onChange` makes every step clickable.

</Framework>

## Accessibility

### ARIA

- Steps and Step components support `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. The Steps root and a Step that is not clickable have no semantic role of their own, so when a name is passed they get `role="group"` and the name reaches the accessible name; without a name no `role` is added. The fill, basic and nav types behave the same
- The current step has `aria-current="step"`; other steps do not
- When Steps listens for step changes or a Step has a click handler, steps are focusable with Tab and their `role` defaults to `button` (taking precedence over `group`; an explicit `role` wins over both). `Enter` or Space on a focused step acts like a mouse click and runs both the Step's click handler and the change callback of Steps; steps that are not clickable are not focusable
- A click that comes from another focusable element inside the step (`input`, `button`, a link, and so on) runs only the Step's click handler, not the change callback of Steps. A clickable step is itself `role="button"`, so a focusable control in the title or description is a nested interactive element; do not make such a step clickable

## API reference

### Steps

The whole step bar.

<Framework only="react">

<StepsReactApi />

`aria-labelledby`, `id` and other native attributes land on the root element (`div`) of Steps.

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

<StepsVueApi />

`class`, `style` and native attributes are passed through attrs and land on the root element of Steps.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

<StepsSolidApi />

`class`, `style`, `ref` and other native attributes land on the root element of Steps.

</Framework>

### Steps.Step

Step in the step bar.

<Framework only="react">

<StepReactApi />

`aria-label`, `role`, `onKeyDown` and other native attributes land on the step item (the root element). `role` defaults to `button` when the step is clickable, and a passed `role` takes precedence; `onKeyDown` is called for every key. When the step is clickable, `Enter` or Space on a focused step item acts like a click.

</Framework>

<Framework only="vue">

<StepVueApi />

`class`, `style`, `aria-*`, `role`, `tabindex` and native attributes and events such as `@click` and `@keydown` are passed through attrs and land on the root element of the Step. A `@click` from the caller makes that Step clickable; `onChange` of Steps runs before the Step's own `@click`. `tabindex` can be overridden by the caller.

</Framework>

<Framework only="solid">

<StepSolidApi />

`class`, `style`, `aria-*` and native events such as `onClick` and `onKeyDown` land on the root element of the Step (the step item). `role` follows whether the step is clickable, and a passed `role` takes precedence; `tabindex` also follows whether the step is clickable and cannot be overridden.

</Framework>

## Content Guidelines

- Step title
    - title should be kept concise, avoiding truncation and line breaks
    - use sentence capitalization
    - do not include punctuation
- Step description
    - supplementary contextual information for the title
    - don't end with punctuation
